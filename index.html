 Raymond
The Tuckaway Tavern and Butchery
★★★★★Jason's Rating

One of New Hampshire’s most beloved spots — Chef Bobby ‘The Butcher’ Marcotte runs an unforgettable kitchen. The only establishment in New England that publicly displays their entire menu of fresh ingredients in the adjacent butchery. Award-winning steaks, creative burgers, lobster and more — all made with top quality ingredients and a chef’s creative spin. Get there early — it’s wildly popular and the wait can be significant if you don’t.
