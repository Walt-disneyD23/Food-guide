How alerts work
The switches
Notifications
Master on/off for phone alerts. Off means total silence.
Apply to all rides
Uses your default rule for every ride in the parks, not just the ones you've starred. Can get chatty.
Setting your alerts
Default rulebelow30min
Your baseline — ping me when a wait drops below (or rises above) the minutes you choose. Every starred ride uses this unless you give it its own.
Custom ruleCustomize
Tap Customize on any ride to give just that ride its own number.
Star a ride★
Tap the star on any ride to watch it. It pings once when the wait first crosses your rule, then sits quietly with a ✓ Hit mark and the time. It keeps watching: if the ride goes down and comes back, it re-arms and can alert you again. Stays on your list until you remove it.
Notify meNotify me
On a ride that's down, get a single ping the moment it reopens — then it parks under Back up and goes quiet. (A starred ride, by contrast, keeps watching after it's back.)
