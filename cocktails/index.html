Cucumber Elderflower Gimlet

★★★★★my rating
🍸 Gin🌿 Herbal🍋 Citrusy🌋 Shaken🟢 Easy

A garden-fresh riff on the Gimlet — gin and lime get a floral lift from elderflower and a cool, savory crunch from muddled cucumber, shaken hard and served up. Hendrick's, with its cucumber-and-rose botanicals, is the natural pour here.

🍸 Martini🥒 Cucumber round
 Ingredients 
  • 1.5 oz gin
  • 1 oz elderflower liqueur (St-Germain)
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • 1 (2-inch) piece peeled cucumber, cut into ¼-inch pieces
 Method 

Fill a shaker with ice. Add the gin, elderflower liqueur, lime juice, and cucumber pieces, seal tightly, and shake hard for about 30 seconds. Strain into a martini glass and garnish with a cucumber round.

Hendrick's suits this best — its cucumber and rose-petal notes echo the drink. A classic dry gin only if you really love juniper, or sub vodka.

Tom Collins / Gin Fizz

★★★★★my rating
🍸 Gin🍋 Citrusy🥂 Bubbly🟢 Easy

Two riffs on the same fizzy lemonade-for-grown-ups: gin, lemon, and sugar shaken and lengthened with soda. A Tom Collins goes tall over ice; a Gin Fizz goes into a chilled glass with no ice, for a softer, frothier pour.

🥤 Collins / Highball🍋 Lemon slice
 Ingredients 
  • 2 oz gin
  • 1 oz fresh lemon juice
  • 1 oz simple syrup
  • Soda water, to top
 Method 

Shake the gin, lemon juice, and simple syrup with ice. For a Tom Collins, strain into an ice-filled Collins glass and top with soda. For a Gin Fizz, strain into a chilled highball with no ice and top with soda. Either way, garnish with a lemon slice.

The only real difference is ice and volume. For an egg-rich fizz, dry-shake first: add a whole egg (Royal), egg white (Silver), or yolk (Golden).

Bee's Knees

★★★★★my rating
🍸 Gin🍯 Sweet🍋 Citrusy🟢 Easy

A Prohibition-era classic built on honey — gin, lemon, and honey syrup shaken and served up. Elegant in its simplicity; the honey rounds the lemon into something golden and easy.

🍸 Coupe🍋 Lemon twist
 Ingredients 
  • 2 oz gin
  • 0.75 oz rich honey syrup
  • 0.75 oz fresh lemon juice
 Method 

Shake the gin, honey syrup, and lemon juice with ice. Strain into a chilled coupe and garnish with a lemon twist.

Rich honey syrup is just honey stirred with a little warm water (about 3:1). Swap the gin for white rum (Honeysuckle) or dark rum (Honeybee).

Dirty Martini

🍸 Gin🥃 Spirit-forward🌀 Stirred🟢 Easy

The savory martini — gin (or vodka) stirred ice-cold with a whisper of dry vermouth and a good splash of olive brine. Briny, bracing, and best served so cold it stings.

🍸 Martini🫒 Green olives
 Ingredients 
  • 2.5 oz gin (or vodka), preferably chilled
  • 0.5 oz dry vermouth
  • 0.5 oz green olive brine
 Method 

Chill a martini or coupe glass in the freezer for at least 5 minutes. Combine the gin, dry vermouth, and olive brine in a mixing glass with ice and stir vigorously until well-chilled, about 30 seconds. Strain into the chilled glass and garnish with olives.

Works just as well with vodka — use whichever you like. More brine makes it dirtier; Castelvetrano, manzanilla, or Spanish queen olives all make a good garnish.

Pegu Club

🍸 Gin🍋 Citrusy☕ Bitter🌋 Shaken🟢 Easy

A forgotten classic worth remembering — gin sharpened with orange curaçao, fresh lime, and a double hit of bitters, shaken and served up. Dry, aromatic, and bracingly elegant.

🍸 Martini🍋 Lime twist (optional)
 Ingredients 
  • 1.5 oz gin
  • 0.67 oz orange curaçao or triple sec
  • 0.33 oz fresh lime juice
  • 2 dashes Angostura bitters
  • 2 dashes orange bitters
 Method 

In an ice-filled cocktail shaker, combine the gin, orange curaçao, lime juice, and both bitters. Shake, then strain into a chilled martini glass. Serve neat or with a lime twist.

For a Jasmine riff: cut the curaçao to ¼ oz, swap the lime for ¾ oz fresh lemon juice, and add ¼ oz Campari — a blush-pink, bittersweet cousin.

Toasted Apple Cider Bourbon Smash

★★★★★my rating
🥃 Whiskey🍒 Fruity🍯 Sweet❄️ Winter🟡 Intermediate

A cozy holiday smash — toasted bourbon and orange liqueur stirred into caramel-apple cider, sweetened with maple and dusted with cinnamon sugar. Finished with a toasted cinnamon stick, rosemary, and apple slices, it's autumn in a glass.

🥃 Rocks🍎 Cinnamon-sugar rim · cinnamon stick · rosemary · apple
 Ingredients 
  • 1.5 oz toasted bourbon
  • 0.5 oz orange liqueur
  • 1 tbsp pure maple syrup
  • 0.75 cup caramel apple cider
  • 1 dash cinnamon sugar
 Method 

Stir the bourbon, orange liqueur, maple syrup, cinnamon sugar, and caramel apple cider with ice. Strain into a cinnamon-sugar-rimmed rocks glass over fresh ice. Add apple slices, then a lightly toasted cinnamon stick and a rosemary sprig.

Calls for "toasted bourbon" — Basil Hayden Toast is the recommended bottle. Toasting the cinnamon stick and rosemary over a flame brings out the aromatics.

Whiskey Sour

🥃 Whiskey🍋 Citrusy🌋 Shaken🟢 Easy

The benchmark sour — whiskey, lemon, and a little sweetness held in perfect tension. A touch of egg white makes it silky with a cloud-white cap, and a few dashes of bitters add depth. Rocks or coupe, your call.

🥃 Rocks or coupe🍊 Orange slice + cherry
 Ingredients 
  • 2 oz bourbon or rye whiskey
  • 0.75 oz fresh lemon juice
  • 0.75 oz simple syrup
  • 0.5 oz egg white (optional)
  • 2 dashes Angostura bitters (optional)
 Method 

In an ice-filled shaker, combine the whiskey, lemon juice, simple syrup, and — if using — the egg white and bitters. Shake well, then strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice (or a coupe). Garnish with an orange slice and a cherry on a pick.

Variations: float ¾ oz red wine for a New York Sour; swap the whiskey for amaretto (and skip the simple syrup) for an Amaretto Sour; use rich honey syrup for a Gold Rush.

Whiskey Smash

★★★★★my rating
🥃 Whiskey🌿 Herbal🍋 Citrusy🌋 Shaken🟢 Easy

The Sour's leafy cousin — muddled mint and bourbon brightened with lemon and a little sweetness. Herbal, refreshing, and dead simple once you've coaxed the oils out of the mint.

🥃 Rocks🌿 Mint sprig
 Ingredients 
  • 6 large mint leaves
  • 0.5 oz simple syrup
  • 2 oz bourbon
  • 0.5 oz fresh lemon juice
 Method 

Muddle the mint leaves with the simple syrup in a shaker to release the oils. Add ice, the bourbon, and lemon juice, and shake until well chilled. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice and garnish with a mint sprig.

Drop the lemon and build it in a julep cup over crushed ice and you've got a Mint Julep. Rye, other whiskey, brandy, or dark rum all work in place of the bourbon.

Original Mai Tai

🍹 Rum🥥 Tiki🍋 Citrusy🌋 Shaken🟡 Intermediate

Trader Vic's 1944 original — the purist's Mai Tai, with no pineapple or orange juice in sight. It leans entirely on good aged rum, fresh lime, orange curaçao, and almondy orgeat over crushed ice. As the story goes, a Tahitian friend took one sip and called it "maita'i" — out of this world.

🥃 Rocks (crushed ice)🌿 Lime shell + mint
 Ingredients 
  • 2 oz aged rum
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • 0.5 oz orange curaçao
  • 0.5 oz orgeat syrup
  • 0.25 oz rich simple syrup
 Method 

Shake the rum, lime, curaçao, orgeat, and simple syrup with crushed ice, then pour unstrained into a rocks glass. Garnish with a spent lime shell and a mint sprig.

For the rum, a blend of 1 oz amber Martinique + 1 oz dark Jamaican is classic. No orgeat? Amaretto stands in, a touch sweeter. Fresh lime only.

Hawaiian Mai Tai

★★★★★my rating
⭐ House favorite🍹 Rum🥥 Tiki🍒 Fruity🍋 Citrusy🟢 Easy

The island-resort take on the Mai Tai — sweeter, fruitier, and built for the beach. Pineapple and orange juice join the rum, orgeat, and orange liqueur, with a float of dark rum crowning the glass. Less austere than the 1944 original; more of a sunset-on-the-lanai pour.

🥤 Tall glass🍒 Cherry + pineapple spear
 Ingredients 
  • 1.5 oz light rum
  • 1 oz dark rum (floated)
  • 1.5 oz pineapple juice
  • 0.75 oz orange juice
  • 0.5 oz orange liqueur
  • 0.5 oz orgeat syrup
 Method 

Shake the light rum, pineapple juice, orange juice, orange liqueur, and orgeat with ice, then pour over crushed ice in a tall glass. Float the dark rum on top, and garnish with a maraschino cherry and a pineapple spear.

Orange juice is to taste — anywhere from ½ to 1 oz. Float the dark rum last for the dramatic dark cap.

Fog Cutter

★★★★★my rating
🍹 Rum🥥 Tiki🍋 Citrusy🍒 Fruity🟡 Intermediate

An over-the-top tiki monster that hides its strength behind the fruit — white rum, brandy, and gin all pile in with orange, lemon, and almondy orgeat, finished with a float of nutty amontillado sherry. Strong as anything; sip slowly.

🥥 Tiki mug / Collins🌿 Mint sprig + straw
 Ingredients 
  • 1.5 oz white rum or rhum agricole
  • 0.5 oz brandy
  • 0.5 oz gin
  • 2 oz fresh orange juice
  • 1 oz fresh lemon juice
  • 0.5 oz orgeat
  • 0.5 oz amontillado sherry (float)
 Method 

Shake the rum, brandy, gin, orange juice, lemon juice, and orgeat with ice. Pour into a tiki mug with crushed ice (or an ice-filled Collins glass) and float the sherry on top. Garnish with a mint sprig and serve with a straw.

For more punch and less juice, replace the orange juice with ½ oz orange curaçao.

Mojito

★★★★★my rating
🍹 Rum🌿 Herbal🍋 Citrusy🥂 Bubbly🟢 Easy

The easygoing benchmark cooler — muddled mint, white rum, and lime, lengthened with soda over crushed ice. Smooth, minty, and endlessly drinkable on a warm afternoon.

🥤 Highball🌿 Mint sprig + straw
 Ingredients 
  • 6 large mint leaves
  • 0.75 oz simple syrup
  • 0.75 oz fresh lime juice
  • 2 oz white rum
  • Soda water, to top
 Method 

Muddle the mint leaves in the bottom of a shaker. Add the simple syrup, lime juice, and rum, and shake. Strain into a highball glass with crushed ice and top with soda. Garnish with a mint sprig and serve with a straw.

A dash or two of Angostura is a popular addition; ginger, passion fruit, or pineapple are all nice in the muddle. Top with dry sparkling wine instead of soda if you're feeling fancy.

Piña Colada

🍹 Rum🥥 Tiki🍒 Fruity☀️ Summer🟢 Easy

The original frozen escape — light rum blended with pineapple, cream of coconut, and lime until thick and snowy. Pure beach in a glass, parasol and all.

🥥 Hurricane🍍 Pineapple + cherry + parasol
 Ingredients 
  • 0.5 cup diced pineapple, frozen
  • 2 oz rum (light or aged)
  • 1.5 oz cream of coconut
  • 1 oz pineapple juice
  • 0.5 oz fresh lime juice
  • Pinch of salt
 Method 

Freeze the pineapple ahead if needed. In a blender, combine the frozen pineapple, about 1 cup of ice, the rum, cream of coconut, pineapple juice, lime juice, and salt. Blend until smooth. Pour into a hurricane or pint glass — or a hollowed-out pineapple if you're feeling it — and garnish with the cherry, a pineapple wedge, and a parasol. Serve with a straw.

Any pineapple works since it's blended — fresh, frozen, or drained canned (canned-in-syrup runs sweeter). Cream of coconut isn't the same as coconut milk or cream, so grab the right can.

El Pepino

🌵 Tequila🍋 Citrusy🔥 Spicy🌋 Shaken☀️ Summer🟢 Easy

"El pepino" is Spanish for cucumber, and this cool-but-fiery Margarita riff earns the name. Muddled cucumber and fresh jalapeño give it a garden-fresh body and a slow, creeping heat, with lime, agave, and a chili-salt rim keeping it bright and balanced.

🥃 Rocks🌶️ Chili-salt rim + cucumber
 Ingredients 
  • 2 ¼-inch wheels cucumber
  • 2 ¼-inch wheels fresh jalapeño
  • 1.5 oz blanco tequila
  • 0.75 oz agave syrup
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
 Method 

Muddle the cucumber, jalapeño, and tequila in a shaker for about 10 seconds, until fully pulverized. Add the agave and lime, fill with ice, and shake 15–20 seconds until well-chilled. Double-strain through a fine-mesh strainer into a Tajín-rimmed rocks glass over fresh ice, and garnish with a cucumber slice.

The heat scales with the jalapeño — seed the wheels for a milder version.

Spicy Margarita

★★★★★my rating
⭐ House favorite🌵 Tequila🔥 Spicy🍋 Citrusy🌋 Shaken🟢 Easy

A Margarita with a kick — Ancho Reyes chili liqueur brings a smoky, ancho-pepper heat to the usual reposado-and-lime backbone. Agave keeps it rounded, and a chile-lime salt rim doubles down on the warmth.

🥃 Rocks🌶️ Chile-lime salt rim + lime peel
 Ingredients 
  • 1 oz reposado tequila
  • 1 oz Ancho Reyes chili liqueur
  • 0.75 oz fresh lime juice
  • 0.5 oz agave syrup
 Method 

Shake with plenty of ice, then double-strain into a rocks glass. Garnish with a lime peel or wedge.

🌿 Lighter Version · Verde
  • 1 oz blanco tequila
  • 1 oz Ancho Reyes Verde (poblano) liqueur
  • 0.75 oz fresh lime juice
  • 0.5 oz agave syrup
Built exactly the same way — shake hard with ice and double-strain over fresh rocks. Swapping blanco for the reposado and the green poblano Verde for the original ancho trades the roasted, smoky heat for something brighter and grassier, with more fresh-pepper lift. Same chile-lime rim.

A half salt rim is a nice touch — salted sips on one side, clean on the other.

Paloma

🌵 Tequila🍋 Citrusy🥂 Bubbly☀️ Summer🟢 Easy

Tequila's easygoing everyday cooler — lime and a pinch of salt stretched long with grapefruit soda over ice. Bright, gently bitter, and impossibly refreshing in the heat.

🥤 Highball🍋 Lime wheel
 Ingredients 
  • 2 oz tequila
  • 0.5 oz fresh lime juice
  • 4 oz grapefruit soda, chilled (to top)
  • Pinch of salt
 Method 

Add the tequila, lime juice, and a pinch of salt to a tall glass and fill with ice. Top with the grapefruit soda and stir briefly and gently to combine. Garnish with a lime wheel.

Grapefruit soda like Jarritos or Squirt is traditional — or use fresh grapefruit juice with a splash of club soda and a little extra lime.

Tequila Sunrise

🌵 Tequila🍋 Citrusy🍒 Fruity☀️ Summer🟢 Easy

A sunset in a glass — orange juice and tequila poured over a pool of grenadine so the color bleeds from gold to red. A breezy '70s throwback that's all about the looks and the ease.

🥤 Collins🍊 Orange wheel + cherry
 Ingredients 
  • 0.25 oz grenadine
  • 1.5 oz tequila
  • 3 oz orange juice
 Method 

In a chilled Collins glass, add the grenadine, top with ice, and set aside. In an ice-filled shaker, combine the tequila and orange juice and shake. Gently strain into the Collins glass so the layers stay distinct. Garnish with the orange wheel and cherry.

Find it too sweet? Swap the grenadine for ½ oz crème de cassis (fruity and tart) or Campari (bittersweet).

Blueberry Lavender Vodka Spritzer

★★★★★my rating
🧊 Vodka🍒 Fruity🌿 Herbal🥂 Bubbly🎉 Party🟡 Intermediate

A pitcher-made summer cooler built on a homemade blueberry-lavender syrup — vodka, fresh lime, and club soda do the rest. Floral and berry-bright without being sweet, it's the kind of thing you batch ahead and pour all afternoon.

🍶 Pitcher · serves 8🫐 Blueberries + lavender
 Ingredients 
  • 2 cups vodka
  • 0.75 cup blueberry-lavender syrup
  • 4 oz fresh lime juice
  • 1 liter club soda
  • 1 cup blueberries
 Method 

In a glass pitcher, stir together the vodka, blueberry-lavender syrup, lime juice, and club soda. Add ice and a handful of blueberries. Pour into glasses and garnish each with a few blueberries and a lavender sprig.

Just one? Single serving
  • 2 oz vodka
  • 1 tbsp + 2 tsp lavender syrup
  • ½ oz lime juice
  • 2 tbsp blueberries
  • ~4.5 oz club soda
Build over ice in a tall glass — vodka, syrup, and lime — then top with club soda. Add the blueberries and a lavender sprig.

Blueberry-lavender syrup (make ahead): simmer 1 cup water, 1 cup sugar, and 1 cup blueberries with 4 fresh lavender sprigs (or 1 tbsp dried) for ~10 minutes, then strain, mashing the berries. It makes more than one batch needs — keep the extra on hand. Using dried lavender? Use 1½ tsp food-grade buds in place of the sprigs.

Lemon Drop

🧊 Vodka🍋 Citrusy🍯 Sweet🌋 Shaken🟢 Easy

A sweet-tart crowd-pleaser — vodka and triple sec shaken with plenty of fresh lemon and poured into a sugar-rimmed glass. Lemonade with a backbone.

🍸 Coupe🍋 Sugar rim
 Ingredients 
  • 2 oz vodka
  • 0.5 oz triple sec
  • 1 oz fresh lemon juice
  • 1 oz simple syrup
 Method 

Coat the rim of a cocktail glass with sugar and set it aside to dry — a few minutes ahead is ideal so it adheres well. Add the vodka, triple sec, lemon juice, and simple syrup to a shaker with ice and shake until well-chilled. Strain into the prepared glass.

Rim the glass a few minutes ahead so the sugar dries and sticks. Ease back on the simple syrup if you like things more tart than sweet.

Espresso Martini

🧊 Vodka☕ Bitter🍫 Rich/Boozy🌙 Nightcap🟡 Intermediate

The dinner-party showstopper — vodka, fresh espresso, and coffee liqueur shaken hard until it pours with a frothy cap. Bittersweet, wide awake, and hard to stop at one.

🍸 Coupe☕ Coffee beans (optional)
 Ingredients 
  • 2 oz vodka
  • 1 oz brewed espresso, cooled (or cold brew concentrate)
  • 0.5 oz coffee liqueur
  • 0.5 oz simple syrup
 Method 

Combine the vodka, espresso, coffee liqueur, and simple syrup in a shaker. Add ice and shake very hard — the hard shake is what gives you that signature foam cap. Strain into a chilled coupe, garnish with a few coffee beans, and serve right away.

Cool the espresso completely before shaking, or it'll melt the ice and thin the drink — a few minutes in the freezer speeds things up. For a creamy version, swap one ounce of vodka for Irish cream; decaf makes a gentle after-dinner pour.

Cosmopolitan

🧊 Vodka🍋 Citrusy🍒 Fruity🌋 Shaken🟢 Easy

The icon of the '90s, still a good time — vodka shaken with triple sec, fresh lime, and a splash of cranberry, strained out pretty and pink. Tart, bright, and unfussy.

🍸 Martini🍋 Lime wedge
 Ingredients 
  • 1.5 oz vodka (plain or citron)
  • 0.75 oz triple sec
  • 0.75 oz fresh lime juice
  • 1 oz cranberry juice
 Method 

In an ice-filled cocktail shaker, combine the vodka, triple sec, lime juice, and cranberry juice. Shake, then strain into a chilled martini glass (or an ice-filled rocks glass). Garnish with the lime wedge.

Easy riffs: swap the vodka for tequila with a couple dashes of Angostura, or trade the cranberry for pomegranate juice.

Mississippi Punch

🍇 Brandy🍋 Citrusy🍫 Rich/Boozy🎉 Party🟢 Easy

A deceptively strong sipper from the 1860s — brandy out front, with bourbon and dark rum stacked behind it over lemon and a little sugar. It drinks easy and hits hard; a proper party-starter.

🥤 Collins🍋 Citrus slice + seasonal berries
 Ingredients 
  • 1 tsp superfine sugar
  • 0.5 oz fresh lemon juice
  • 2 oz brandy
  • 1 oz bourbon
  • 1 oz dark rum
 Method 

Stir the sugar and lemon juice in a shaker until the sugar dissolves. Add the brandy, bourbon, and dark rum with ice and shake. Strain into an ice-filled Collins glass and garnish with a citrus slice and a few seasonal berries.

Traditionally made with cognac, but any decent brandy does the job beautifully.

Aperol Spritz

🥂 Spritz☕ Bitter🍋 Citrusy☀️ Summer🟢 Easy

Italy's golden hour in a glass — bittersweet Aperol lengthened with dry sparkling wine and a splash of soda over ice. Light, fizzy, and just bitter enough; the quintessential warm-weather aperitivo.

🍷 Wine glass🍊 Orange slice
 Ingredients 
  • 3 oz dry sparkling wine
  • 2 oz Aperol
  • 1 oz soda water
 Method 

Fill a wine glass with ice. Add the sparkling wine, Aperol, and soda, and stir gently to combine. Garnish with an orange slice.

Swap the Aperol for another Italian amaro — Cappelletti, Montenegro, even Pimm's — for a different spritz. Or replace the sparkling wine with ginger ale, rhubarb soda, or bitter lemon.

Negroni Sbagliato

🥂 Spritz☕ Bitter🍋 Citrusy🌀 Stirred🟢 Easy

The “mistaken Negroni,” and arguably the happier accident — Campari and sweet vermouth lengthened with prosecco instead of gin. All the bittersweet depth of the original, lighter on its feet and dressed in bubbles.

🥃 Rocks🍊 Orange slice
 Ingredients 
  • 1 oz Campari
  • 1 oz sweet vermouth
  • 2 oz prosecco
 Method 

Build over ice in a rocks glass: add the Campari and sweet vermouth and stir to combine. Top with the prosecco and give it one gentle stir to keep the bubbles. Garnish with an orange slice or twist.

The “mistaken Negroni” — prosecco stands in for the gin. Pour the prosecco a touch heavier for a lighter, fizzier drink.

Hugo Spritz

🥂 Spritz🌿 Herbal🍋 Citrusy☀️ Summer🟢 Easy

The Aperol Spritz's floral cousin — prosecco and elderflower lifted with soda, fresh mint, and a squeeze of lime. Delicate, garden-fresh, and barely sweet; summer in a wine glass.

🍷 Wine glass🌿 Mint + lime
 Ingredients 
  • 1 oz elderflower liqueur
  • 3 oz prosecco
  • 2 oz soda water
 Method 

Fill a wine glass with ice and add a few mint leaves and a lime wedge. Pour in the elderflower liqueur, then the prosecco, then top with soda. Stir gently and garnish with more mint and lime.

Clap the mint between your hands rather than muddling, so it stays fragrant and not bitter. A few berries or cucumber slices are a nice touch.

Garibaldi

🥂 Spritz☕ Bitter🍒 Fruity🍳 Brunch🟢 Easy

Two ingredients, one little ritual — bitter Campari and a wave of orange juice whipped until it's light and fluffy. Bittersweet, sunny, and somehow more than the sum of its parts.

🥤 Highball🍊 Orange wedge
 Ingredients 
  • 1.5 oz Campari
  • 4 oz fresh orange juice (aerated until fluffy)
 Method 

Aerate the orange juice until light and frothy — a blender or handheld frother does it. Fill a highball glass with ice, add the Campari, then pour the fluffy juice over the top. Garnish with an orange wedge.

The fluffy juice is the whole trick — aerating it softens the bitterness and gives a delicate foam. A tiny pinch of salt makes the orange taste sweeter.
